SigmaGo

Architecture v2

Seven views, outside in. Updated for the STEP dependency chain, the platform plane, approval stance capture, and post-decision confidence.

Changed since v1
  • New view 03 — the STEP chain. STEP is now a dependency structure, not a flat taxonomy. This is the largest change.
  • Data model gains chain fields, stance and outcome on approval steps, and the platform plane.
  • Lifecycle gains a chain-reference stage at raise and a confidence stage after sealing.
  • Modules reduced to five; Delegations and Access moved inside.
  • Deployment gains observability and the mandatory test-database guard.

03The STEP chain

New in v2. STEP is no longer a flat taxonomy. Each decision references the one it rests on, forming a directed acyclic graph rooted in Structural decisions.

STRUCTURAL · root
Competency Framework
references nothing — or supersedes a prior version

must reference a Structural decision

TRANSACTIONAL

follows the rule

Promotion outside the boundary
bends the rule
Two directions, two questions. Looking down gives Impact Factor and Blast Radius — what rests on this. Looking up gives Inherited Soundness — what this rests on, and whether any ancestor is unreasoned, superseded, or unreviewed.

The diagnostic that falls out: if a requester cannot name the Process decision their Transaction rests on, either the rule was never recorded — organizational inheritance, detected at the moment somebody acts on it — or it is not a Transaction at all but an Exception, because no rule governs it.

05Decision lifecycle

Two stages are new in v2: chain reference at raise, and confidence after sealing.

01
Raise & anchor
Category chosen. Requester names the rule this rests on. Two-sided case required on exceptions.
02
Route
Path from category config. entered_at stamped per stage.
03
Contextualise
Exception count, Impact Factor, prior precedent, inherited soundness.
04
Decide
Endorse or approve with reservation. Outcome and stance captured.
05
Seal
SHA-256 over content, chain, reasoning, participants.
06
Score
Confidence computed after sealing and frozen. Never before.
07
Execute & reuse
Actioned, then becomes precedent for the next similar decision.
Stage 06 is deliberately after stage 05. A confidence score shown before the decision would anchor it. Computed after sealing, it becomes a property of the record rather than an input to judgment — and it goes on the certificate, permanently. A decision approved with one reservation and incomplete evidence should say so forever.

Participation runs alongside every stage. Comments are preserved on the certificate, marked non-authoritative. A participant can never advance the request.

Three fields cannot be backfilled and must be captured from the first day of use: stance, outcome, and parent_reference_id. You cannot ask a past approver whether they had reservations, nor reconstruct which rule a past transaction was following.

Frozen at seal: confidence_at_seal, soundness_at_seal, blast_at_seal. Live figures change as descendants accumulate; the certificate records what was true at the time.

07Deployment and operations

Colocation matters more than any query optimisation. Observability is new in v2.

Application
Next.js on Vercel · functions pinned to bom1 to colocate with the database
Database
Supabase Postgres ap-south · RLS on every table · pooler on 6543, direct 5432 for migrations
Identity
One identity system — the certificate's authority proof requires a single source of truth for who a person is
Secrets
AES-256-GCM at rest, rotation-versioned · write-only in every API response
Sealing
SHA-256 over a versioned canonical form · deterministic, no salt, so a third party can verify independently
Observability
Structured JSON logs with tenant and request IDs · Sentry with a reasoning scrubber · uptime to phone · seal failure pages immediately
Integrations
Provider-agnostic contracts for metrics, alerts, logs and errors · Grafana, OpsGenie, PagerDuty, Slack, generic webhook
Testing
Unit suite in-memory · integration suite against real Postgres behind a mandatory TEST_DATABASE_URL that must differ from primary
Environments
Production · staging with synthetic data only · never a production copy
Alerts and logs leave our infrastructure. They carry IDs only — never tenant names, decision subjects, reference strings, person names, or reasoning. An alert naming a compensation decision would place customer data in third-party SaaS, possibly in an SMS.

Determinism is what makes the certificate meaningful. Any randomness in the digest would make independent verification impossible, and "verifiable by anyone without access to SigmaGo" would be false.
